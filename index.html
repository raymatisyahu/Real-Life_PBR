📚 Slide 1: Concept Review

There are three important terms in percentage problems.

Percentage (P)

The part or amount being considered.

Base (B)

The whole or total amount.

Rate (R)

The percent used to compare the part with the whole.

Important Formulas

P = B × R
B = P ÷ R
R = P ÷ B

Remember: Change the percent into decimal form before using the formula.
